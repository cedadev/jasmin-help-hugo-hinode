## JASMIN Accounts Portal

The {{<link "jasmin_accounts_portal">}}JASMIN Accounts Portal{{</link>}} is where you manage your JASMIN account, apply for access to JASMIN services such as SSH access, and if you are a service manager or deputy, manage access to those services.

{{<alert alert-type="info">}}
If you have an existing account but are unable to access JASMIN for any reason (e.g., forgotten password, changed email address or institution), **please do not reregister for a new JASMIN account**. If you have already created a new account, please do not apply for any services with the new account. Instead, [please contact the helpdesk](https://jasmin.ac.uk/help/contact/) to resolve the issue.
{{</alert>}}

## Apply for a new JASMIN account

To apply for a JASMIN account you need to use an email address affiliated with your academic institution. This is the preferred option — otherwise your application may take longer to be approved. Proceed as follows:

**Step 1**: Go to the {{<link "jasmin_accounts_portal">}}JASMIN Accounts Portal{{</link>}} and
select "Apply for a new JASMIN account".

{{<image src="img/docs/get-jasmin-portal-account/jasmin-accounts-welcome.png" caption="JASMIN Accounts Portal">}}

**Step 2**: Fill in the form with supporting information for your application, so the JASMIN Team can assess your eligibility for a JASMIN account. More information about eligibility can be found on {{<link "https://jasmin.ac.uk/users/access/">}}our Access page{{</link>}}.

|  Field Name  | Information |
|-----|---------|
|  Email | The email address associated with your institution (workplace, university, etc.).  |
|  First name | Your first name/given name.  |
|  Surname | Your last name/family name.  |
|  Discipline | The primary discipline you work in. |
|  Degree | If you are currently working towards a degree, the type of degree. Otherwise, select "Not studying for a degree".  |
|  What will you be using JASMIN for? | Helpful information to specify here includes:<br> &bull; The name of the project you want to use JASMIN to work on<br> &bull; The reference code of any grant you may have to work on this project<br> &bull; The source(s) of funding for the project<br> &bull; The expected start and end dates of the project<br> &bull; The name of any JASMIN Group Workspace(s) currently used for the project<br> &bull; Links to the project homepage<br>**If you are applying from an institution outside of the UK**, please also specify the names and email addresses of two contacts in the UK who could act as references  |
{.table-striped .table-bordered .border-primary}

**Step 3**: Press **Submit application** at the bottom of the page.

{{<image src="img/docs/get-jasmin-portal-account/application-email-verification.png" caption="Application submitted successfully">}}

**Step 4**: After pressing **Submit application**, follow the verification link sent to your email address.

**Step 5**: Once your email has been verified, you will receive a second email with the subject 'Application approved' inviting you to finish setting up your account.

{{<alert alert-type="info">}}
Depending on the institution you belong to, the 'Application approved' email may take a few working days to arrive. Please use an email address affiliated with your academic institution, rather than a personal email.
{{</alert>}}

The link in the 'Application approved' email will take you to the following page where you choose your JASMIN account credentials and register your [SSH public key]({{% ref "generate-ssh-key-pair" %}}). After you have completed the form, click **Create account** at the bottom of the page.

{{<image src="img/docs/get-jasmin-portal-account/application-credentials.png" caption="Choose credentials and add public key">}}

**Step 6**: Agree to the {{<link "jasmin_tcs">}}JASMIN Terms and Conditions of Access{{</link>}}:

{{<image src="img/docs/get-jasmin-portal-account/application-terms.png" caption="JASMIN Terms and Conditions of Access">}}

**Step 7**: Your JASMIN account has been created and you can log into the Accounts Portal using your credentials:

{{<image src="img/docs/get-jasmin-portal-account/application-complete.png" caption="Account creation complete">}}

{{<alert alert-type="info">}}
Please note that registering for an account does not by itself provide you with any access to JASMIN machines or services. If you want to connect to JASMIN, you will need to [request access to the `jasmin-login` service]({{% ref "get-login-account" %}}).
{{</alert>}}
